# Platypi Arcade: Support

Platypi Arcade is a collection of 39 retro arcade, card and dice games by Ice-Type Platypi Studios, for iPhone and Android.

**Contact:** icetypeplatypistudios@gmail.com. Please include your phone model and what happened, and we'll reply as soon as we can.

## Common questions

**How do I remove the ads?**
Open **Settings** from the hub (bottom left) and tap **REMOVE ADS**. It's a one-time purchase that turns off the ads between games for good. The optional "Watch ad: double tickets" button stays, and you never have to use it.

**I bought Remove Ads on another phone, or reinstalled the game.**
Open **Settings** and tap **RESTORE PURCHASE** while signed in to the same Apple Account or Google account you bought it with.

**Can I change my ad privacy choices?**
Yes. On iPhone, go to the phone's **Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking**. In the European Economic Area, the UK, Switzerland and US states with privacy laws, use **PRIVACY CHOICES** in the game's Settings. Saying no still shows ads, just not personalised ones.

**Where is my progress saved?**
Only on your phone: tickets, prizes, trophies and games in progress. There are no accounts, so progress can't move between phones, and deleting the game deletes it.

**Does it need the internet?**
The games all play offline. The internet is only used for ads and for the Remove Ads purchase.

**Can I buy tickets?**
No. Tickets are only won by playing, and they can't be bought or cashed out.
