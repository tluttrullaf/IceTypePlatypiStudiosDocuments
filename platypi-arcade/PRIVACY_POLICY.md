# Platypi Arcade: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026. Publisher: Ice-Type Platypi Studios ("we"). This policy covers Platypi Arcade on iPhone, iPad and Android.

## In short

Platypi Arcade has no accounts and we run no servers, so we never receive your personal information. The game is free and shows ads, supplied by Google AdMob. Google may collect some device data to show and measure those ads, as explained below. A one-time "Remove Ads" purchase turns off the ads between games.

## What stays on your device

Your progress (tickets, prizes, trophies, settings and games in progress) is saved only on your device. It is never sent to us, and uninstalling the app deletes it.

## Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows a full-screen ad between games now and then (never during play), and an optional ad you can choose to watch to double the tickets you just won. These ads come from Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. To serve ads, limit how often you see them, measure them and prevent fraud, the AdMob software in the app may collect and send to Google:

- your device's advertising identifier (on iPhone and iPad only if you allow tracking when asked; on Android the Android advertising ID, which you can reset or delete in your device settings), and an app set ID that Google uses for analytics and fraud prevention;
- your IP address, which can show your approximate location (country or city), and basic device information such as model, operating system and language;
- how you interact with the app and the ads (for example, app launches, and whether an ad was shown, watched or tapped), and diagnostic data such as crash logs, performance and ad loading errors.

How Google uses this data is described at [policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites) and in Google's privacy policy at [policies.google.com/privacy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). We do not receive this data ourselves; we only see totals from Google, such as how many ads were shown.

## Your choices about ads

- **Tracking on iPhone and iPad.** The app asks before any tracking. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", the advertising identifier is not used and you still see ads, but they are not personalised. You can change this any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
- **European Economic Area, United Kingdom and Switzerland.** The app asks for your consent before personalised ads, using Google's consent form. You can change your answer any time from PRIVACY CHOICES in the game's Settings.
- **United States.** Where state privacy laws give you the right to opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information for targeted advertising, you can do so from PRIVACY CHOICES in the game's Settings.
- **Android.** You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's settings (Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads).
- **Remove Ads.** The one-time purchase turns off the ads between games. The optional "watch an ad to double tickets" button stays available, and you never have to use it.

## Purchases

The "Remove Ads" purchase is handled entirely by the Apple App Store or Google Play. We never see your name, payment details or account. The store only tells the app that the purchase was made, so the app can switch off ads and restore the purchase on a new device.

## Children

Platypi Arcade is meant for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, and the ads are limited to content suitable for teenagers.

## Sharing and selling

We do not sell your personal information, and we hold none to share. The only data that leaves your device is the advertising data described above, which goes to Google to provide the ads.

## Changes

If a future version changes any of this, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will change.
